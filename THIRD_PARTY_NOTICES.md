# Third-party notices

The N1 Go module has no third-party module requirements. Its compiled Go
executables include Go runtime/standard-library material under the Go Authors'
BSD-style license. The full text from the pinned Go 1.25.12 toolchain is retained
in [LICENSES/Go-LICENSE](LICENSES/Go-LICENSE).

This statement covers the N1 source dependency graph and the identified Go
notice. External compilers, CI actions, operating systems and separately
installed software retain their own licenses; they are not relicensed by the
root Apache-2.0 file. Recheck the actual contents when distributing a new build.

Follow [the historical distribution instructions](docs/LICENSING.md) so these
materials accompany the unchanged N1 binaries and source archive.
